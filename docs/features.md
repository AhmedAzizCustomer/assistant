# Features Guide

## AI-Powered Chat Assistant

The chat interface provides a conversational way to interact with all features of your personal assistant.

### What You Can Ask:

- "What's on my schedule today?"
- "Show me my high-priority tasks"
- "Help me plan meals for this week"
- "Suggest a workout for today"
- "When is my next meeting?"
- "Draft a response to my last email"
- "Find time for a 30-minute task"

### How It Works:

The AI has context about your:
- Current tasks and their priorities
- Upcoming calendar events
- Recent meals and nutrition goals
- Workout history and fitness goals
- Schedule constraints

## Task Management

### Creating Tasks

Tasks can have:
- Title and description
- Priority level (low, medium, high, urgent)
- Category (work, personal, health, etc.)
- Due date
- Estimated duration

### AI Features:

1. **Task Breakdown**: Complex tasks can be automatically broken down into subtasks
2. **Smart Insights**: AI analyzes tasks to suggest:
   - Estimated completion time
   - Best approach
   - Potential challenges
   - Optimal time of day to work on it

3. **Priority Suggestions**: AI can help determine task priorities based on deadlines and dependencies

## Email Management

### Supported Providers:
- Gmail
- Outlook

### Features:

1. **Email Analysis**: AI analyzes incoming emails for:
   - Priority level
   - Category (work, personal, promotional, etc.)
   - Sentiment
   - Suggested actions

2. **Smart Drafting**: Generate email responses by:
   - Providing context or instructions
   - Choosing tone (professional, casual, formal, friendly)
   - AI drafts appropriate response

3. **Inbox Summary**: Get AI-powered summaries of your inbox with:
   - High-priority emails requiring immediate attention
   - Categorized emails
   - Action items

## Calendar & Events

### Integration:
- Google Calendar
- Outlook Calendar

### Features:

1. **Event Sync**: Automatically sync events from connected calendars

2. **Availability Check**: Check if time slots are available

3. **Smart Scheduling**: Find optimal times for meetings considering:
   - Existing commitments
   - Work hours
   - Preferred meeting times

4. **Conflict Detection**: Automatic detection of scheduling conflicts

## Schedule Planning & Optimization

### AI Schedule Optimizer

The AI can optimize your entire schedule considering:

1. **Tasks**: Scheduled based on:
   - Priority and deadlines
   - Estimated duration
   - Dependencies
   - Your energy levels throughout the day

2. **Fixed Events**: Meetings and appointments that can't be moved

3. **Workouts**: Optimal times for exercise based on:
   - Your energy patterns
   - Available time blocks
   - Recovery needs

4. **Meals**: Meal times scheduled to:
   - Maintain consistent eating schedule
   - Avoid conflicts with important tasks
   - Support nutrition goals

5. **Breaks**: Automatic inclusion of break time for:
   - Mental rest
   - Physical activity
   - Meals

### Work-Life Balance

The optimizer considers:
- Working hours boundaries
- Time for personal activities
- Rest and recovery
- Family time

## Meal Planning & Nutrition

### AI Meal Planning

Generate personalized meal plans that consider:
- Dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.)
- Calorie targets
- Macro nutrient ratios (protein, carbs, fat)
- Food preferences
- Cooking time available

### Features:

1. **Meal Logging**: Track what you eat with nutritional information

2. **Nutritional Analysis**: AI estimates nutrition from meal descriptions

3. **Weekly Plans**: Generate complete weekly meal plans with:
   - Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
   - Recipes and preparation instructions
   - Nutritional breakdown
   - Grocery lists

4. **Progress Tracking**: Monitor:
   - Daily calorie intake
   - Macro nutrient balance
   - Nutritional goals progress

## Workout Planning & Fitness

### AI Workout Planning

Generate personalized workout plans based on:
- Fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- Goals (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, flexibility)
- Available time
- Equipment access

### Features:

1. **Workout Logging**: Track workouts with:

2. **Workout Plans**: AI-generated plans include:
   - Weekly schedule

   - Fitness improvements

## Privacy & Security

- All data is encrypted in transit
- OAuth 2.0 for third-party integrations
- No data sharing with third parties
- Secure storage of credentials
- You control what data is synced

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Specific**: When asking questions, provide context
   - Instead of: "Help me plan"
   - Try: "Help me plan my week focusing on completing the marketing project"

2. **Regular Updates**: Keep your tasks, meals, and workouts logged for better AI insights

3. **Set Preferences**: Configure your preferences for:
   - Working hours
   - Meal times
   - Workout preferences
   - Dietary restrictions

4. **Review AI Suggestions**: The AI provides suggestions - you make the final decisions

5. **Use Chat for Complex Requests**: The chat interface can handle multi-step requests better than individual features
